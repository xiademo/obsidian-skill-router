# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project respectful and harassment-free
for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or viewpoint.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and specific.
- Assume good intent while evaluating evidence critically.
- Accept responsibility, correct mistakes, and respect project boundaries.
- Keep private data, credentials, and local Skill content out of public reports.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory conduct, deliberate disclosure of private
information, and sustained disruption are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns through the repository's private
[security-advisory form](https://github.com/xiademo/obsidian-skill-router/security/advisories/new)
and begin the title with `Code of Conduct`. Do not post sensitive evidence in a
public issue. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily
or permanently restrict participation when needed to protect the community.

Enforcement decisions should be proportionate, documented privately, and made
without retaliation against good-faith reporters.
